01 / Who we are

We don't just book space on ships. We board them.

TAS began as a shipping agency on the Butterworth waterfront, meeting vessels at all hours and getting them in, worked and out again. That habit of being physically there, on the quay and on the bridge, still shapes how the group handles every container, air waybill and truck that passes through its hands.

Today four companies share one standard: know the port, own the problem, and keep the customer informed before they need to ask.

    07 / Heritage

    Five decades on the waterfront.

    1. 1978

      Founded in Butterworth

      TAS opens on the mainland side of the Penang Channel, serving the ships calling at Penang Port.

    2. 1980s

      On board, at every berth

      The agency builds its name the hard way: boarding at all hours, clearing fast, fixing what goes wrong.

    3. 1990s

      From ships to shippers

      Forwarding and customs clearance bring the same discipline to the cargo inside the containers.

    4. 2000s

      Becoming a group

      Agency, Maritime, Freight Services and Management Holdings sail as one group under shared standards.

    5. 2010s

      Multimodal by default

      Sea, air and cross-border road tied together, so a shipper deals with one desk, not four.

    6. Today

      Gateway to the world

      From one Butterworth office to lanes reaching Europe, the Gulf, North Asia and Australia.

    7. Next

      The next horizon

      Digital quoting, live visibility and lower-carbon routing. The next fifty years start here.
